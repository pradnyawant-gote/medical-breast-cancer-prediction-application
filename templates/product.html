WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES01 — 03
01

Accepts your image.

Upload a PNG or JPEG breast ultrasound image up to 10 MB. The selected image stays visible beside the result.

02

Matches model input.

The app converts the image to RGB, resizes it to 128 × 128, and scales pixel values by 1/255 as in the training notebook.

03

Shows every score.

View the predicted class and the scores for benign, malignant, and normal together.
